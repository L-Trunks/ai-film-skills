# 预告片（3 镜快切 + 旁白）

预告片节奏范例，镜数曲线 1-3-3-1。
两个要点：① 旁白必须声明画外音，否则模型会安排人对镜头念；
② 题材避开模型没有训练数据的东西（异兽本体），只拍它留下的痕迹与后果。

> 实测配置：4 段 / 8 镜 / 20.7 秒 / 画幅 (736, 416)

---

## 段 0（1 镜）

```
A hand holding a phone up toward a mountain ridge at dusk, searching for signal. The camera slowly pushes in on the screen as the last bar of signal disappears and the screen dims to black, mist closing in behind the ridge. A male Mandarin Chinese voiceover says: “第三天，信号没了。” The narrator is never visible and no one on screen is speaking or moving their lips. Anamorphic widescreen, low saturation high contrast, 35mm film grain, cold overcast daylight, no dramatic lighting, no text or captions anywhere.
```

---

## 段 1（3 镜）

```
A 3-shot sequence with hard cuts between shots. SHOT 1: Enormous weathered rib bones jutting from the valley floor, taller than trees, vines swaying between them, absolutely no man-made structures. HARD CUT. SHOT 2: A giant feather standing upright in wet earth, taller than a man, its vane rippling. HARD CUT. SHOT 3: A huge three-toed footprint filled with rainwater, raindrops landing and rippling. A male Mandarin Chinese voiceover says: “山里有别的东西。” The narrator is never visible and no one on screen is speaking or moving their lips. Anamorphic widescreen, low saturation high contrast, 35mm film grain, cold overcast daylight, no dramatic lighting, no text or captions anywhere.
```

## 段 2（3 镜）

```
A 3-shot sequence with hard cuts between shots. SHOT 1: A wide swath of ancient trees snapped and flattened in one direction, the break lines fresh and pale. HARD CUT. SHOT 2: A shallow pool of water trembling in concentric rings, as if something heavy is walking nearby, nothing visible. HARD CUT. SHOT 3: A man pressed into a rock crevice, only half his face showing, eyes flicking sideways, his mouth firmly closed, holding his breath. A male Mandarin Chinese voiceover says: “它不躲人。” The narrator is never visible and no one on screen is speaking or moving their lips. Anamorphic widescreen, low saturation high contrast, 35mm film grain, cold overcast daylight, no dramatic lighting, no text or captions anywhere.
```

## 段 3（1 镜）

```
Valley mist parting slowly to reveal layer after layer of impossible mountains beyond, ridge behind ridge fading into distance. The camera holds completely still as the mist opens and then begins to close again. A male Mandarin Chinese voiceover says: “回来以后，我再没提过那座山。” The narrator is never visible and no one on screen is speaking or moving their lips. Anamorphic widescreen, low saturation high contrast, 35mm film grain, cold overcast daylight, no dramatic lighting, no text or captions anywhere.
```
